## Research focus

My work examines EMS as a health system, with particular attention to the organizational, geographic, educational, and financial conditions that shape service availability and sustainability.

Current areas of focus include:

- **EMS workforce:** retention, turnover, job satisfaction, work patterns, and organizational culture
- **Financing and policy:** reimbursement, revenue capture, payment design, and policy mechanisms affecting service capacity
- **Education and access:** program performance, geographic access to EMS education, and structural differences in educational opportunity
- **Systems and operations:** national EMS utilization, service delivery, behavioral health demand, operational variation, and system performance

## Updating the site

Most content updates can be made directly in `index.html`.

Visual and responsive changes are handled in `styles.css`.

Changes committed to the `main` branch are automatically deployed through GitHub Pages.
